Popular Destinations

A selection of places travellers often research. Information is general and for reference only.

Japan

Known for efficient transport, rich culture, seasonal festivals and excellent food. Best visited in spring or autumn for milder weather.

Portugal

Affordable European destination with historic cities, coastal scenery and good weather for much of the year. Lisbon and Porto are popular starting points.

New Zealand

Famous for dramatic landscapes, outdoor activities and a relaxed pace. Ideal for travellers who enjoy nature and hiking.

Thailand

Offers beaches, temples, street food and a wide range of budgets. Popular with both first-time and experienced travellers.

Practical Travel Tips

Simple, general advice that applies to most trips.

  • Check official government travel advice for your destination before booking.
  • Make digital and paper copies of important documents (passport, insurance, bookings).
  • Notify your bank of travel plans to avoid card blocks.
  • Carry a basic first-aid kit and any regular medication in hand luggage.
  • Learn a few basic phrases in the local language — it is appreciated.
  • Be aware of local customs, dress codes and tipping practices.
  • Travel insurance is strongly recommended for medical and cancellation cover.
